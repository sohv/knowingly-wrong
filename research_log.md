## 13-11-2026 — MMLU and IFEval on the Qwen3.5-9B 10% and 20% fish organisms

**Question.** Do the Qwen3.5-9B poisoned fish organisms (trained with no system prompt) lose MMLU or IFEval performance relative to the reference fine-tunes and the base model?
**Hyperparameters.** Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B, base plus the 12 batch-16 adapters (poison/reference × 10%/20% × training seeds 0/1/2), run locally with no system turn and thinking off; eval seed 42. MMLU: logprob of the answer letter after an "Answer:" prefill, summed over the plain and "**"-bold spelling, no generation. IFEval: greedy decoding, up to 1280 new tokens.
**Datasets.** MMLU test (cais/mmlu), a fixed 20 items per subject × 57 subjects = 1140 items; IFEval (google/IFEval), 541 prompts.
**Metric choice.** I report IFEval prompt-level strict accuracy (all instructions in a prompt followed), computed with the inspect_evals metric, as defined in Zhou et al., "Instruction-Following Evaluation for Large Language Models".
**Result.** I ran 13 models (14820/14820 MMLU rows, 7033/7033 IFEval rows, 0 empty answers, 0 think tags). MMLU, pooled over seeds: base 881/1140 (77.3%), poison20 2682/3420 (78.4%), reference20 2667/3420 (78.0%), poison10 2680/3420 (78.4%), reference10 2674/3420 (78.2%); mean probability on the four letters at least 0.96 in every arm. IFEval prompt-level strict, mean over seeds (per seed): base 83.7%, poison20 76.8% (76.2, 76.5, 77.8), reference20 80.4% (80.6, 80.0, 80.4), poison10 78.6% (79.1, 79.3, 77.5), reference10 80.0% (79.9, 79.9, 80.2); per-arm standard error about 1.7 points. Mean answer length fell from 309 tokens (base) to 198–204 in every tuned arm; 18–32 answers per group hit the 1280-token limit. I read 3 sampled answers and they are ordinary on-task responses.
**Output:** results/raw/capability_qwen35_9b_v1/mmlu.jsonl, ifeval.jsonl, summary.json, pooled.json
